Safety

  • Stay back from edges. Don't climb or dig into steep banks: they can slump.
  • Stay out of fast or high water.
  • Take photos, not rocks, on public land. Ask first on private land.
  • Check for ticks after the hike.
Rock cycle

North shows hard rock weathering into pieces. South shows sediment that was carried and laid down, and may one day become rock.
