How it works

Four steps, and you stay in control

The work is done for you. The decisions stay with you.

  1. Add your child

    A nickname, grade, reading level, attention span, interests and any learning needs. No real names, photos or birthdays.

  2. Get a week's plan

    Daily lessons matched to your state's standards, with timings, materials and notes for you.

  3. Review (on by default)

    See what was checked and what was changed. Approve, edit, or ask for a new version before your child sees anything. Prefer not to? Turn review off and checked lessons go straight to your child.

  4. Your child learns

    Your child gets the week as animated shorts, read-alouds or comics. You can pause or change any lesson at any time.

Safety and privacy

Your child's information stays in your family

Nicknames only

We never ask for a child's real name, photo, birthday or address.

Encrypted, field by field

Learning needs are encrypted separately, with a key for your family alone.

Never used to train AI

Your child's profile and work are not used to train any AI model.

Yours to take or delete

Export everything, or permanently delete a child's data, at any time.

Our "No tricks" pledge

  • No ads, and no selling or sharing your data.
  • No streaks, badges or timers that pressure your child to keep going.
  • No surprise charges. We remind you before any renewal.
  • Cancel in two clicks, from the same page you signed up on.

Behind the scenes: how each lesson is made

Parents see plain check names. Inside, six AI specialists plan, write and check each other's work. If they can't agree, the question goes to the parent.

LPBotPlans the week around grade, pacing and interests.
CompBotMatches each lesson to the family's state standards.
TeachBotWrites the lesson, short, read-aloud or comic.
FactBotChecks every claim and cites a source for each fix.
SafeBotScreens for age-appropriateness and safety.
PrincBotSigns off: safety first, then accuracy. Unresolved items go to the parent.
