What you can make

One topic. Your child's interests. Three ways to learn it.

Pick a subject and a format. Every piece is written for one child's grade, reading level and attention span.

Short animations

60 to 120 second explainers with captions, narrated at your child's reading level.

Example: a soccer-loving 6th grader learns why the Moon has phases through a stadium-lights analogy.

Podcasts

Two friendly hosts talk through the topic. Episode length follows your child's attention span.

Example: a horse-crazy 8th grader hears linear equations explained through feed costs at a stable.

Web comics

Short illustrated stories that star characters built on what your child loves.

Example: a dinosaur fan in 3rd grade follows a dino detective reading clues in fossils.

How every piece is made

Five AI specialists check each other's work

Nothing reaches your child until it has been planned, aligned, written, fact-checked and screened for age-appropriateness. Anything they can't agree on comes to you to decide.

Product tour

From a child's profile to a finished lesson

Follow one family through My Classroom. Each step opens the live demo screen.

1

Add a Virtual Child

A parent adds a nickname, grade, reading level, attention span, learning needs and interests. Up to 10 children and 3 adult users per subscription.

2

Generate a lesson plan

LPBot builds a week of lessons. CompBot tags each day with the matching state standard, for example NJSLS-S 3-LS4-1 on fossils.

3

Create personalized content

Choose a topic and format. Watch the agents plan, write, fact-check and screen it, then see exactly what was corrected and why.

4

Track progress against standards

Record graded work and level-setting tests, then see which standards are covered, in progress or not yet started.

5

Parents have the final say

When the agents can't settle something, it lands in the review queue with a plain explanation.
